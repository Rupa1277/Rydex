## 3. Project information — use this as the factual basis

**Problem statement:** Two-wheeler riders are vulnerable to road accidents, especially when travelling alone or in areas where immediate assistance may not be available. After an accident, injuries or unconsciousness may prevent riders from contacting emergency services or sharing their location with family members. This can delay necessary assistance.

**Proposed solution:** Rydex is a compact IoT-based accident detection and emergency alert system.

- ESP32 microcontroller for device control and Bluetooth connectivity.
- MPU6050 motion sensor to identify sudden movement or tilt patterns that may indicate a possible accident.
- Bluetooth connection between the device and an Android mobile application.
- A confirmation countdown that gives the rider an opportunity to cancel a false alert.
- The application uses the phone's location and available communication services to notify selected emergency contacts.
- Design priorities include affordability, ease of use and practicality for everyday riders.
